## Retrospective

The 16-versus-8 mismatch was not a display-only defect. Desktop and mobile had separate interpretations of Next membership and a full-state replacement protocol that could erase or overwrite valid work. The durable fix was to define one domain invariant, move authority into a transactional store, and make every client reconcile explicit entity mutations against it.
